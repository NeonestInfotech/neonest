About Us

Delivering quality software to businesses worldwide

Neonest Infotech started in 2022 with a small team focused on web design. With a futuristic vision and untiring energy, the company grew quickly — and today our 20+ action-driven team members deliver complete digital solutions for startups and enterprises.

We build Android & iOS apps, custom and e-commerce websites, Unity games, custom software and UI/UX design, and we take care of app publishing and digital marketing — almost everything under the hood of computing and communication technology.
